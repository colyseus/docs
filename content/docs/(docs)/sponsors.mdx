Colyseus is an independent project: no venture capital, no corporate parent. One
developer maintains all of it full-time, from the server and the schema
serializer to the SDKs, the documentation, and the demos.

The framework is MIT licensed and free to self-host, and it stays that way. No
paid plan, no gated feature. Your support is simply what buys the time to keep
all of it moving.

## Ways to support Colyseus

There are two ways to help, and both mean a lot <HeartFillIcon/>

### GitHub Sponsors

The most direct one: sponsor monthly or once, at
[github.com/sponsors/endel](https://github.com/sponsors/endel). Monthly sponsors
get the source code of the [premium demos](/learn#premium-demos) from $5 up.
Higher tiers add priority on bug fixes, email support, and a logo listing.

### Colyseus Merch

Want something in return? The [Colyseus Shop](https://shop.colyseus.io/) has
t-shirts, hoodies, hats, mugs, and stickers, and it ships worldwide. Every order
funds the work, and you get to look cool doing it. 😎
