# Authentication

Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user. Colyseus provides built-in tools to help you authenticate your clients into your application.

The fastest path is the built-in [`@colyseus/auth`](/auth/module) ↔ [`@colyseus/database`](/database) integration: pass a `GameDatabase` to `defineServer` and the database becomes the user store, all auth routes mount automatically, and JWTs become revocation-aware. See [Database → Authentication](/database#authentication-built-in-colyseusauth-integration).

You can also bring your own token validation logic, or wire [<PackageIcon/> Auth Module](/auth/module/) against your own database.
